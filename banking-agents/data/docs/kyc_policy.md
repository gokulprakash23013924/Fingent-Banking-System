# KYC and AML Policy

## KYC validity
A customer's KYC must be marked verified and must have been verified within the last 365 days. Pending or expired KYC means loan applications and large transfers are held until the customer completes re-verification at a branch or through video KYC.

## AML screening
A customer flagged by AML screening cannot be given credit or make large transfers. Staff do not disclose the screening outcome to the customer; the case is referred to the compliance team.

## Large transfers
Transfers of 50,000 rupees or more need verified KYC and an explicit customer approval before the transfer is executed.
