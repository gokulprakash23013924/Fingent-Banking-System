# Personal Loan Policy

## Eligibility
A personal loan applicant must have a credit bureau score of at least 650. Applicants below this score are declined. The rate depends on the score: 10.5% a year for 750 and above, 12% for 700 to 749, and 14% for 650 to 699.

## Affordability
The sum of existing monthly obligations and the new EMI must not exceed 50% of the applicant's monthly income. This ratio is called the obligations-to-income ratio, also known as FOIR. When a request exceeds the limit the bank offers a smaller amount that fits.

## Amount and tenure
Loan amounts range from 50,000 to 25,00,000 rupees. The amount cannot exceed 20 times the applicant's monthly income. Tenure ranges from 6 to 84 months.

## Application
An application is created only after the customer confirms it. Applications are held for manual review when KYC is incomplete or when recent transactions show elevated fraud risk.
